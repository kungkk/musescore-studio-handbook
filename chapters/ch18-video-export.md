# Chapter 18: Video Export

## Core Idea
MuseScore Studio can render a score-following video: the score scrolls/zooms along with playback, exported as MP4. Useful for YouTube covers, rehearsal tracks, and online sharing.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Video export**:
  - Publish tab → Export → choose Video (MP4).
  - Options: resolution, frame rate, zoom/scroll behavior, include audio.
  - Renders score as it plays back; camera follows the playback cursor.
- **Rehearsal videos**: pair with metronome on, export with audio.
- **Audio-only**: export WAV/MP3 (see Ch 16).

## Key Takeaways
1. Video export renders score-following MP4.
2. Publish→Export → Video.
3. Pairs with playback settings (metronome, repeats).
